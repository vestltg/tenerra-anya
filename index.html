Anya is an AI for special needs parents — including parents of children with intellectual and developmental disabilities (IDD) — designed to hold and make clear what a parent knows about their child. Unlike general AI tools, Anya focuses on one person — your child — and helps ensure that knowledge remains usable for care planning, transitions, and long-term continuity.

Anya was created by Michael Pearce, an attorney with 30 years of experience in intellectual and developmental disabilities law. It is offered by Tenerra, Inc. at anya.tenerra.ai with a 7-day free trial and an annual plan of $199 per year (about $16.60 per month).

How Anya turns your knowledge into something you can use.

1. Tell

Talk to Anya naturally. Tell her what you know about your child — the routines, preferences, communication, triggers, and little things that matter. You don't have to organize it first. Just tell her.

2. Build

Anya helps organize what you share into a clear, living understanding of your child. You can keep adding to it as you learn more, as things change, and as your child's life changes.

3. Use

When the time comes, the knowledge you've built can help the people supporting your child understand them more deeply. So they're not starting from zero.

When someone else needs to know.

Life changes. A new caregiver arrives. A familiar caregiver leaves. Your child moves into a new setting. Something unexpected happens. Someone needs to understand your child — and they don't have the years you've had.

That's when the knowledge you've built matters.

Anya doesn't decide who should receive it. You do.

The Knowing Will Continue.

Your knowing doesn't have to end with you. One day, someone else may become a primary caregiver for your child. They won't know what you know — not at first. But they can start with what you've learned.

And as they come to know your child, they can add what they learn — what works now, what's changed, what they've discovered. Then, when another caregiver steps in, they don't have to start over.

The knowledge keeps growing. The knowing continues.

We want to be straight with you.

Anya is not a therapist. Anya is not a doctor. Anya is not an attorney.

Anya is a tool for capturing and developing the knowledge you have about your child.

Private. Secure. Yours.

Your child's story belongs to you. Anya doesn't sell your child's information. It isn't used to train other AI systems. And Anya doesn't share your child's knowledge with anyone. You decide when someone else needs to know — and how you choose to share what you've built.
